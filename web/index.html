Bounded clearing rules

One cutoff, one disclosed method

01

Funded limits

Buyers escrow their maximum payment. Sellers escrow their full DEMO-EQUITY quantity. Orders can be cancelled and refunded before cutoff.

02

One capped scan

The program accepts at most 32 orders and 101 consecutive one-cent candidate prices. The opening reference is a disclosed test input.

03

Deterministic price

Maximize matched quantity, then minimize imbalance, then choose the nearest opening reference, then the lower price.

04

Claims or refunds

Filled orders settle at one price. Unfilled balances are claimable. Failed clearing halts to refunds; a permissionless abort opens after 30 minutes.

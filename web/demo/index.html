    Choose the verified market

  2. 02
    Prepare Devnet SOL and test assets

  3. 03
    Create the auction account

    Review the exact market test pair and cutoff, then sign the account-creation transaction.

Step 2 · joiner

What happens from the shared market URL?

The URL contains the selected product symbol and one auction address. The server and browser recheck the official product mapping, program owner, exact test mints, state, and deployed bounds before showing actions.

  1. 01
    Confirm the market and pair

    Read the official page and exact CallWindow test mint. No real PreStocks token enters the auction.

  2. 02
    Connect Phantom or Solflare on Devnet

    Use Devnet SOL for fees and rent. Claim the selected test assets up to two times per wallet if the capped distributor is available.

  3. 03
    Fund a limit order

    Buyers escrow DEMO-USD. Sellers escrow the selected market test shares. Review the exact action before signing.

  4. 04
    Watch the provisional result

    Funded interest and the candidate clearing price can change while orders are added or cancelled before cutoff.

  5. 05
    Close and settle

    After cutoff, close the account. Each order owner then claims a match or refunds the unused escrow.

This is a replayable Devnet test-asset mechanism. The $19.50–$20.50 grid is illustrative only and does not represent the selected PreStocks market price, fillability, or execution.
